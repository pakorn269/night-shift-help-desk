# Product Steering — Night Shift Help Desk

## Category & Design Thesis
- **Kiroween Category**: **Costume Contest**
- **Core Premise**: Make the player doubt ordinary enterprise IT service-desk software before introducing anything visibly supernatural. The interface *is* the haunted object.
- **Narrative Arc**: Three escalating night-shift incidents separated by seven minutes (`03:00` → `03:14` → `03:21`):
  1. `INC-0300`: Workstation powered on by itself at `03:00`, with an impossible `03:07` closure under the player's own name.
  2. `INC-0314`: RFID badge swipe `#0419` (the player's own badge) at the empty Floor 4 North Stairwell.
  3. `INC-0321`: Shift handover request submitted by the player's own extension (`Ext. 419`) citing pre-dated decisions from `02:55`.
- **Player Expression**: Players can personalize their Analyst Name in the header so their own name appears on impossible future timestamps and audit entries. Their compliance vs. defiance pattern unlocks 4 persistent Shift Endings (`The Quiet Shift`, `Extension 419`, `Split Session`, `Audit Exception`).

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails
- No gore, sudden jump scares, strobing flashes, or audio-only clues.
- Every critical clue must remain legible on a 360 px mobile viewport without hover, audio, or color-only signaling.
- Zero real company names, real endpoints, backend services, or paid APIs.
